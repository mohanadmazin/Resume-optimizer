# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ResumeAI to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements constructively.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the code, documentation, or proposal.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community constructive.

For concerns about conduct, contact the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue containing sensitive details.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants are representing or participating in the ResumeAI community.
